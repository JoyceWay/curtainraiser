开学：我的 AI 学习计划

Day 0 of learning AI in public. The goal: a one-person AI business. The method: spiral learning — revisit, deepen, ship.

1. 我是谁

背景

46 岁，二十多年前拿的 CS 学位，中间在上海做过三年市场研究（数据分析，Excel / VBA）。现在重学 AI，目标不是找工作，是建立自己的王国。

46, CS degree from twenty years ago, three years of market research in Shanghai (data analysis, Excel/VBA). Relearning AI now — not for a job, but to build my own kingdom.

2. 目标

目标

月入 $50k，每周工作不超过 10 小时，80% 被动收入。听起来很远，所以拆成三步：先学透，再做出东西，最后赚到钱。

$50k/month, ≤10 hours a week, 80% passive income. Far away — so three steps: learn deeply first, then build, then earn.

3. 方法：螺旋式学习

方法

同一个概念，过几遍，一遍比一遍深。第一遍跑通，第二遍加深，第三遍做出东西。不追求一遍学会——小步快走，摩擦力小才能坚持。

Spiral learning: revisit the same concept in passes, each deeper than the last. Pass one: get it running. Pass two: deepen. Pass three: ship. Small steps, low friction, sustainable.

4. 这个网站是干什么的

网站

记录我的学习过程和总结。写给小白看——包括过去的我自己。能把难的东西讲简单，才算真学会。

This site records my learning process and summaries, written for beginners — including my past self. If you can explain hard things simply, you've truly learned them.
