10 Talks, Ranked for Me

YC Startup School 2026 — ten talks, ordered by value to a solo founder building an AI business. English first; Chinese in small text.
YC Startup School 2026 十场对谈，按"对我的价值"从高到低排名。英文在前，中文小字在后。完整逐场简报见 research 文件。
#1

Boris Cherny — 80% of the Work Is the Prompt video

Head of Claude Code at Anthropic. The most directly actionable talk for daily agent work.

Anthropic Claude Code 负责人。对日常 agent 工作最直接的一场。

Strip the system prompt to zero, then add lines back one by one — only what the model repeatedly fails at earns its place. Verification beats prompt engineering; skills beat context (context carries a 15–20% tax).

把 system prompt 删光，逐行加回——只在模型反复栽倒的地方才加。验证大于提示词工程；skill 大于 context（context 有 15–20% 的税）。

Take one: run an ablation pass over my prompt/skill/cron setup — delete first, re-add on evidence.

拿走一句：给我的 prompt / skill / cron 体系做一次 ablation 大扫除——先删，再按证据加回。

#2

Alexandr Wang — Civilization-Scale Opportunity video

Founder of Scale AI, now leading Meta's superintelligence lab. The theoretical source of my loop engineering.

Scale AI 创始人，现领导 Meta superintelligence lab。我 loop 工程的理论源头。已单独深挖。

Evals over prompts. Companies are feedback loops; the alpha is agentic systems that operate them. The bottleneck is diffusion, not model progress — the theoretical backing for my bridge positioning.

eval 优先于 prompt。公司即反馈循环，alpha 在于用 agent 系统运营这些循环。瓶颈是扩散而非模型进步——这是我"桥梁"定位的理论背书。

Take one: every loop review starts with "is the metric defined?" — before touching any prompt.

拿走一句：每次 loop 复盘先问"metric 定了吗"，再碰 prompt。

#3

Garry Tan — Own Your Intelligence video

President & CEO of YC. "Markdown is code" — skill files as owned, executable cognition.

YC 总裁兼 CEO。"Markdown 就是代码"——skill 文件是外化的、可执行的认知资产。

Frontier models are rented and commoditized; your context is owned and uncopyable. Formula: rented model + owned context + agent runtime = a faster you. Skillify what the agent learns into reusable files.

前沿模型是租的、商品化的；你的 context 是拥有的、无法复制的。公式：租来的模型 + 拥有的 context + agent 运行时 = 一个跑得飞快的你。把 agent 学到的东西 skillify 成可复用文件。

Take one: my cron + memory system is the asset — keep compounding it; "write clear English instructions and you're a programmer" backs the no-code-judgment route.

拿走一句：我的 cron + 记忆系统就是资产，继续复利；"会写清晰英文指令就是程序员"背书我的"不学写码、学判断"路线。

#4

Jeff Dean — The 1% Rule for Building in AI video

Chief Scientist at Google. A hard direction filter for choosing what to build.

Google 首席科学家。选方向的硬过滤器。

The 0%–1% vs 20% test: if today's general model already solves 20% of your problem, absorption risk within 6–12 months is real; if it solves 0%–1%, that's the opportunity. When agents write all code, taste becomes the scarce resource — train it by writing down predictions and reviewing a year later.

0%–1% vs 20% 测试：今天的通用模型已能解决你问题的 20%，6–12 个月被吸收是真风险；只能解决 0%–1%，那就是机会。agent 写掉所有代码后，taste 是稀缺资源——把预测写下来、一年后复盘来训练判断力。

Take one: run every direction through the 0–1% test; my bridge/diffusion positioning sits exactly where models are weak.

拿走一句：每个方向都过一遍 0–1% 测试；我的"桥梁/扩散层"定位正好落在模型做不好的地带。

#5

Patrick Collison — Is AI Breaking the Lean Startup Playbook? video

Co-founder & CEO of Stripe. Knowledge as cognitive L1 cache — don't outsource learning prematurely.

Stripe 联合创始人兼 CEO。知识是"认知 L1 缓存"——别过早把学习外包出去。

AI didn't break the lean playbook, it moved the starting line. He's never read a moving LLM-written piece. Start from a real, lived pain (Stripe was saved by one: online payments hurt). Decorrelate: enter from a divergent starting point nobody occupies.

AI 没有打破精益 playbook，只是把起跑线前移。他从没读过一篇打动人的 LLM 文章。从自己活过的真实痛点出发。去相关：从没人占的分歧起点切入。

Take one: "learning stays in-house, execution outsources" — Collison is the authority quote for it.

拿走一句："学习不外包、执行外包"——Collison 是这句话的权威背书。

#6

Sam Altman — Never a Better Time to Found video*

CEO of OpenAI. Conviction against conventional wisdom; build, don't attack. (*Video URL not independently verified.)

OpenAI CEO。对常识有 conviction；建造，不攻击。（*视频链接未独立核实。）

Best founding conditions ever: fast tech change + falling costs + shorter cycles. Find something you can reasonably believe while the mainstream says you're wrong — endure the quiet years. "Mildly helpful to many people" is the right path.

有史以来最好的创业时机。找到一个你能合理坚持、而主流说你错的东西，忍受安静的年份。"对很多人 mildly helpful"是正道。

Take one: my three unoccupied squares (Chinese + AI-learner POV + company of one) are exactly the quiet-years bet.

拿走一句：我的三格空位（中文 + AI 学习者第一视角 + 一人公司）正是这种"安静年份"的赌注。

#7

Jensen Huang — The Mindset of NVIDIA video

Founder & CEO of NVIDIA. Curiosity + hands-on feel; confront reality, learn the fundamentals, rebuild.

NVIDIA 创始人兼 CEO。好奇心 + 手感；直面现实，学基本功，重造。

Being wrong on tech is normal — reaction decides survival. Great companies come from a believed worldview, not the tech itself. Systems thinking is the new code. "You don't have to win the day — win each morning."

选错技术是常态，反应决定生死。伟大公司来自"你深信的独特世界观"。系统思维是新的 code。"不用一天赢下人生，赢下每个早晨就行。"

Take one: "the tech itself doesn't matter; being able to learn it does" — backs judgment-over-coding, daily.

拿走一句："技术本身不重要，学得会才重要"——每天背书"学判断不学写码"。

#8

Max Hodak — Average Is Not Good Enough video

CEO of Science, ex-Neuralink co-founder. Speed comes from infrastructure; iteration rate decides.

Science 公司 CEO，前 Neuralink 联创。速度来自基础设施；迭代速度决定生死。

Deep-tech teams are fast because boring things (procurement, hiring, budgets) run smoothly. Judgment can't be outsourced. When stuck: "action produces information."

深科技团队快，是因为无聊的东西（采购、招聘、预算）跑得顺。判断力不可外包。卡住时："行动产生信息。"

Take one: my version: ship once a week, every week.

拿走一句：我的版本：每周交付一次。

#9

Blake Scholl — How 50 People Built a Supersonic Jet video

Founder & CEO of Boom Supersonic. Hardware story, but the learning method transfers.

Boom Supersonic 创始人兼 CEO。硬件故事，但学习方法可迁移。

Make hardware behave like software: move engineering into code, automated integration tests. Keep a "confusion list" — kill one concept per week you got right but didn't truly understand. "The answer to why now can be because I started now."

让硬件长得像软件。维护"confusion list"——每周消灭一个"我答对了但没真懂"的概念。"why now 的答案可以是'因为我现在开始了'。"

Take one: steal the confusion list for my learning loop; "why now = I started" cures timing anxiety.

拿走一句：抄走 confusion list 放进我的学习循环；"why now = 我开始了"治时机焦虑。

#10

Dmitri Dolgov — The Demo Is Only 1% of the Work video

Co-CEO of Waymo. Furthest domain, but "eval before product" transfers.

Waymo 联合 CEO。领域最远，但"先定 eval 再动手"可迁移。

A demo took 18 months; the product took 15 years. Every extra "nine" of reliability costs 10x. Build the eval before the product — the fuel of the agent/simulator/critic flywheel is the metric.

demo 花了 18 个月，产品花了 15 年。每多一个 9，可靠性成本翻十倍。先建 eval 再建产品——飞轮的燃料是 metric。

Take one: downgrade "how many nines" to: what is the numeric definition of "good enough" for my content/product?

拿走一句：把"几个 9"降级为：我的内容/产品"够好"的数字定义是什么？
